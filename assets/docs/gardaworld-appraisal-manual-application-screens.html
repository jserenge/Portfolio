Performance Appraisal System

A structured, transparent, and auditable process for setting goals, reviewing progress, and closing out the year — built around three roles working in sync.

Employee application Manager application Automated workflow
01 — Introduction

What the system does

The system supports employees defining and tracking goals, managers reviewing and approving submissions, and HR automating workflow initiation and notifications — all in one auditable trail.

E

Employees

Define and track goals through the year, and submit each stage for manager review.

M

Managers

Review each submission, and approve or request changes with comments.

HR

HR / Automation

Automates workflow initiation, status updates, and notifications across the cycle.

02 — Roles & Responsibilities

Who owns what

Employee

  • Complete personal information
  • Define goals at Start of Financial Year
  • Participate in Mid-Year and End-Year reviews
  • Submit appraisals for manager review

Manager

  • Review submitted appraisals
  • Approve or reject with feedback
  • Ensure reviews complete within timelines

System Automation

  • Initiate appraisal cycles
  • Send notifications when action is required
  • Update status based on approvals or rejections
03 — Employee Application

Five sections, one dashboard

Logging in presents the employee with a dashboard covering every stage of the appraisal — from personal details through to a full history of past reviews.

1

Employee Information

View and verify personal and role-related details, reporting lines, and department — before starting any appraisal process.

2

Start FY Goal Setting

Define performance goals, align them with team and organizational priorities, and submit for managerial approval. The manager is notified automatically.

3

Mid Year Review

Reflect on progress, update achievements and challenges, and submit a self-review. The manager may approve or request revisions.

4

End Year Review

Provide a final self-assessment and summarize outcomes — feeding into performance ratings and compensation discussions.

5

My Appraisals

View all current and historical appraisals, track status, and access feedback left by managers.

04 — Manager Application

Queues for every stage

The manager dashboard groups appraisals by stage, so nothing pending gets missed.

Pending Start FY Reviews

Goals awaiting approval, including any that were previously rejected. Click an employee in the left gallery, then Review Goal to approve or reject with comments.

Pending Mid Year Reviews

Progress reviews awaiting feedback or approval. Click the employee, then Review Goal to approve or reject the mid-year update.

Pending End Year Reviews

Final reviews awaiting closure — add comments and save.

All Reviews

A complete, auditable list of every review under the manager's scope, with a print button to print all reviews at once.

05 — Appraisal Workflow

How a cycle moves end to end

1

System triggers initiate the appraisal cycle

2

Employee is notified to complete their appraisal

3

Employee completes and submits

4

Manager is notified to review

5

Manager approves or rejects with comments

6

Employee is notified of the outcome

7

If rejected, employee revises and resubmits

Status definitions

Approved

The appraisal has been accepted and finalized.

Rejected

The appraisal requires revision by the employee.

06 — Notifications

Automatic, at every handoff

Sent via the configured corporate communication channels — for example email or system alerts.

  • ● An appraisal cycle opens
  • ● An employee submits an appraisal
  • ● A manager approves or rejects an appraisal
  • ● Resubmission is required
