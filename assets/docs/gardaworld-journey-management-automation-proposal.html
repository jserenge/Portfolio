01 — EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Executive Summary

From Excel-based coordination to governed Journey Management automation.

GardaWorld Africa currently manages Journey Management operations through Excel-based Case and Financial Trackers. The proposed platform replaces manual spreadsheet dependency with structured lifecycle governance, stronger financial controls, real-time operational and financial dashboards, improved audit readiness, and scalability.

01

Operational Control

Structured lifecycle stages, real-time case visibility, reduced administrative burden, improved coordination.

02

Financial Governance

Finance-controlled vendor selection, price locking, approved-pricing billing, payment aging visibility.

03

Risk Reduction

Driver/vehicle conflict prevention, escalation, delegation, and variation control.

02 — CURRENT CHALLENGES

Current Challenges

Operational, financial, and governance limitations increase with volume.

Manual updates create human-error risk; status tracking relies on colour coding; performance metrics require manual filtering; driver and vendor coordination lacks structured control; revenue, cost, profit, margin, and payment tracking depend on spreadsheet processes; approval, escalation, and audit controls are not system-enforced.

03 — PROPOSED SOLUTION OVERVIEW

Proposed Solution Overview

A governed Journey Management Automation Platform built on Microsoft Power Platform.

The proposed architecture combines a Power Apps Canvas Application, SharePoint structured data, Power Automate workflows, and structured communication governance. Core application areas include case lifecycle management, vendor tendering, Finance approval, driver assignment, billing/payment tracking, and management dashboards.

05 — SCOPE OF WORK — PHASE 1

Scope of Work — Phase 1

Focused delivery with clear boundaries.

In scope: Power Apps Canvas Application; SharePoint data architecture; multi-vendor tendering; Finance-controlled vendor approval; driver/vehicle conflict prevention; dual WhatsApp governance recording; billing/payment tracking; reporting dashboards; six-week implementation; role-based training; UAT & Go-Live; Hypercare. Out of scope: WhatsApp API automation, GPS live tracking, ERP integration, AI vendor scoring, and dedicated vendor portal.

IN

Phase 1 In Scope

Power Apps Canvas Application, SharePoint data architecture, multi-vendor tendering, Finance-controlled approval, conflict prevention, dual WhatsApp governance recording, billing/payment tracking, dashboards, training, UAT, Go-Live and Hypercare.

OUT

Phase 1 Out of Scope

Automated WhatsApp API integration, GPS live tracking, ERP integration, AI-based vendor scoring and a dedicated vendor portal. These may be considered for Phase 2.

06 — GOVERNANCE MODEL

Governance Model

Governance-first controls are built into the workflow.

Finance approves vendor selection; high-value bookings require secondary approval; Billing authorization and payment confirmation are restricted to Finance. Controllers cannot approve pricing; Finance cannot assign drivers; Drivers cannot access financial data; Vendors cannot access client pricing history. Backup approvers, SLA escalation, delegation windows, and logged escalations are included.

07 — IMPLEMENTATION TIMELINE — 6 WEEKS

Implementation Timeline — 6 Weeks

A staged path from foundation through production deployment.

Week 1: requirements, data structure, intake workflow. Week 2: vendor tendering, approvals, thresholds. Week 3: scheduling, conflict detection, WhatsApp governance logging. Week 4: driver interface, billing, notifications. Week 5: dashboards, payment aging, security hardening, optimization. Week 6: UAT, defect resolution, sign-off, production deployment, Hypercare.

08 — EXPECTED OUTCOMES

Expected Outcomes

A controlled, scalable operating platform.

Expected outcomes include structured workflow-driven operations, automated financial calculations, controlled vendor approval, real-time operational dashboards, improved margin visibility, an audit-ready governance framework, and a scalable platform supporting future growth.

09 — FUTURE ENHANCEMENT ROADMAP — PHASE 2

Future Enhancement Roadmap — Phase 2

Potential capabilities beyond the initial implementation.

The proposal identifies GPS live tracking integration, a vendor self-service portal, automated WhatsApp API integration, predictive vendor scoring, advanced financial analytics, and automated SLA monitoring as potential Phase 2 enhancements.
