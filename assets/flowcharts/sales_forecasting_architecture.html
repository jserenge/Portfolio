Predictive Sales Forecasting Architecture

Retail demand forecasts built on Azure Databricks, with automated retraining and business-ready planning outputs.

1 · Ingest

Retail signals

POS sales, product master, promotions, pricing, stock, and calendar data arrive from operational systems.

2 · Prepare

Lakehouse

Databricks transforms raw feeds into quality-checked daily product-store series and reusable features.

3 · Model

Forecast service

SARIMAX provides baseline forecasts while reinforcement learning optimizes replenishment recommendations.

4 · Operate

MLflow & jobs

Experiments, model versions, data freshness, and accuracy metrics are recorded and governed.

5 · Consume

Planning insights

Forecasts feed Power BI and operational APIs for demand planning, inventory, and exception handling.

Automated retraining

Scheduled jobs retrain on the latest demand history, compare models against a holdout window, and promote only candidates that meet quality thresholds.

Closed-loop measurement

Actual sales return to the lakehouse to calculate MAPE, bias, and service-level impact by store and product segment.
