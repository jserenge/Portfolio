Support Copilot RAG Architecture

Grounded support assistance that retrieves approved knowledge before the LLM drafts a cited answer.

Users

Support agent & customer

Teams, web chat, or CRM starts a question and carries the conversation context.

Knowledge ingestion

Approved content

Runbooks, product docs, policies, and resolved cases are chunked, embedded, and versioned.

Orchestration

Copilot + RAG service

Intent routing, authenticated retrieval, prompt assembly, Azure OpenAI response generation, and source citations.

Retrieval

Vector index

Hybrid semantic and keyword search returns relevant, access-filtered passages.

Actions

Human-approved tools

CRM lookup, ticket creation, and workflow actions execute only with scoped permissions.

Safety & quality controls: identity-based access, prompt-injection screening, content moderation, citation checks, audit logs, feedback capture, and human escalation for low-confidence answers.
